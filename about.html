What is COSUP?

COSUP stands for Community Oriented Substance Use Programme. This is a partnership initiative between the City of Tshwane and the University of Pretoria (COPC Unit, Family Medicine) and supported by a network of part-ners.

COSUP was started because of the increase in drug use and the lack of ser-vices for people who use drugs in Tshwane. It helps people who use sub-stances, families and communities to understand and manage drug (substance) use and related risks.

COSUP helps reduce the health risks of drug users and the community. The programme uses the Community Oriented Primary Care (COPC) approach, which is a public health approach that takes healthcare to the people.

When people who use drugs are ready we help them reduce, change or stop their use depending on their goals.Some people may not be able to or want to stop their drug use and this makes them vulnerable to health risks. All people have the right to health services and support without fear of stigma. For people who inject drugs. this includes the right to access sterile injecting equipment to reduce the spread of HIV and other diseases.

Heroin users with high blood pres-sure or diabetes may need medi-cines to help manage their medical condition. This saves lives, reduces risks and improves the lives of peo-ple and the community.
